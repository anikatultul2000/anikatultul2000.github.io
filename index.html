About me

Hi there! I am an Industrial and Production Engineering graduate from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET). I work on human factors and ergonomics, operations research, optimization, data-driven decision-making, and applied machine learning, and I like using these tools on real problems for a positive impact.

Until recently I was a part-time lecturer at BGMEA University of Fashion and Technology, where I taught courses in supply chain management, production and process control, and computer-integrated manufacturing. I'm looking for research opportunities that combine analytical methods with human-centered design, especially work on how people interact with complex systems such as transportation networks.

Apart from research, I enjoy reading, writing, and exploring new places. I also work in animal welfare and volunteer for animal rescue and rehabilitation. I am passionate about using my skills to make a positive impact on society and the environment.
