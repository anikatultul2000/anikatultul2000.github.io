Progress in Disaster Science · Elsevier · Vol. 31 · 2026

An image processing-based hybrid MCDM framework for urban seismic vulnerability analysis and post-earthquake resource allocation: A case study of Dhaka

Anika Tahsin, A. Islam, A. S. Huq

The problem: Dhaka is one of the most densely populated cities in the world and sits in an earthquake-prone region. Planners need to know which areas are most vulnerable, and how relief resources should be divided after a major quake.

The approach: A hybrid framework that uses image processing to extract urban features, then ranks areas by seismic vulnerability with multi-criteria decision-making (MCDM). These rankings guide how post-earthquake resources are allocated across the city.

Why it matters: It gives disaster-management authorities a data-driven way to prioritize preparedness and response.

Stock Price Prediction in the Banking Sector of Bangladesh: An Ensemble LSTM Framework

A. Islam, Anika Tahsin, A. Karim, S. Sharmin

The problem: Bank stocks in an emerging market like Bangladesh are volatile and hard to forecast, but investors and analysts still need reliable short-term predictions.

The approach: An ensemble of Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks, a deep learning model built for sequential data, trained on historical prices of listed Bangladeshi banks. Combining several models makes the forecasts more robust than any single model.

Why it matters: It shows how deep learning can support financial decisions in a market that has received little attention in forecasting research.

Utilizing Fuzzy TOPSIS in Selecting Best Routes for the Package Delivery Service Companies: A Case Study in Dhaka City

K. M. M. Nafiu, Anika Tahsin, A. A. Hasin, A. S. Huq

The problem: Package delivery companies in Dhaka deal with heavy traffic and unpredictable road conditions, so the best route depends on several criteria at once, and some of those criteria are uncertain or subjective.

The approach: Fuzzy TOPSIS, a multi-criteria decision method that handles vague judgments, used to rank alternative delivery routes and choose the best one.

Venue: The Engineers for Transforming Technology-Driven Smart Bangladesh, 61st IEB Convention, May 9–14, 2024, Institution of Engineers, Bangladesh.
